# Sonny's Deep Dive

Browser game: Sonny dives at 10 beaches to find 5 fish and 5 treasure chests per level, fights sharks and eels with throwing knives, grabs heart coins to heal, and beats a unique 20-hit boss on every level to move on.

Bosses: 1 Great White Shark · 2 Giant Octopus · 3 Giant Eel · 4 Giant Jellyfish · 5 Giant Squid · 6 Giant Crab · 7 Giant Anglerfish · 8 Giant Orca · 9 Giant Blobfish · 10 Giant Megalodon.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

Controls: WASD/Arrows move, S at the water to dive, Space/Click throws a knife the way Sonny faces, M mutes music.

Music and sound effects are synthesized live with the Web Audio API.
