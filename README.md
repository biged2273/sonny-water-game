# Sonny's Deep Dive

Browser game: Sonny dives at 10 beaches to find 5 fish and 5 treasure chests per level, fights sharks and eels with throwing knives, grabs heart coins to heal, and beats a 20-hit shark boss to move on. The final beach is guarded by a Giant Octopus.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

Controls: WASD/Arrows move, S at the water to dive, Space/Click throws a knife the way Sonny faces, M mutes music.

Music and sound effects are synthesized live with the Web Audio API.
